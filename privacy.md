<section class="prose narrow">
  <h2>Analytics</h2>
  <p>
    If you accept analytics cookies, this site uses Google Analytics to collect aggregate usage information such as visited pages, approximate location, device/browser details, referral source, and campaign parameters such as <code>utm_source</code>, <code>utm_medium</code>, and <code>utm_campaign</code>.
  </p>
  <p>
    Analytics is disabled by default. Google Analytics is loaded only after you accept analytics cookies.
  </p>

  <h2>Cookies and local storage</h2>
  <p>
    The site stores your analytics choice in local storage so it can remember whether you accepted or declined analytics. If you accept, Google Analytics may set analytics cookies according to Google's own cookie behavior.
  </p>

  <h2>Managing your choice</h2>
  <p>
    You can change your choice by clearing this site's local storage and cookies in your browser, then reloading the page.
  </p>
